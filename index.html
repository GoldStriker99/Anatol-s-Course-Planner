Every ECE course below is placed against the department's own tentative course list for Fall 26 / Winter 27 / Spring 27, and every prerequisite comes from the ECE prerequisites page. The three slots on each row — FWS — show exactly which quarters that course runs. A hollow outline means the course is outside ECE and its availability is assumed, not read.

Two required depth courses are not being offered at all this year: ECE 157A (Communication Systems) and ECE 161C (Signal & Image Processing). Those two depths are flagged accordingly.

Summer 2027 is listed as TBD for every ECE course, so nothing technical is scheduled there — only COMM, Rady and physics courses that reliably run in summer. Anything placed in Fall 2027 or later assumes next year's pattern matches this year's.

Every course used for a major requirement must be a letter grade, C− or better. No P/NP.

How difficulty is scored. Each course carries a 1–5 load rating based on its structure — lab + lecture, heavy proof/math, cleanroom or capstone project, or lecture-only GE. These are not grade statistics: the SunSET dataset lives in a Google Sheet this build could not reach, so no real distributions are folded in yet. Treat the ramp as a considered ranking, not a measurement. A term's load is the sum of its courses; 15+ is a genuinely hard quarter.
